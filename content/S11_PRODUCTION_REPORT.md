## 8. Component (`ui/media/s11/LiarResponses.tsx`)

- **Placement:** in 二, after the response table. The anchor is the paragraph starting "表格是本章依据条目的整理。下面的组件请你选择一种回应".
- **What it does:**
  1. **Assign a value.** The reader tries to give L a value: 真, 假, 既不真也不假 or 既真又假. For each, the component walks through what the five premises force. After all four have been tried it says that, with all five premises kept, none is consistent.
  2. **Pick a response.** Tarski, Kripke, the paracomplete theory, dialetheism, or "not a statement". The five premises are listed with the abandoned one struck through and marked 放弃或限制. The component then says what the response takes L to be and what it costs. A closing line restates revenge, introduced above the anchor.
  - **Grading:** no response is declared right; every one is shown with its cost.
- **Ordering:** the component sits before the Arabic tradition, so the "not a statement" row is unnamed, as in the table. A draft that named Ṭūsī and Dawānī was corrected during validation. Those names appear only later, and Ṭūsī in fact holds the no-truth-value view. Kripke's "groundedness", explained just after the anchor, is not used.
- **Saved state:** `philopilot.v2.s11.media.liar-responses` (the current value, the values tried, the response). 重新开始 clears everything.
- **Checked in the browser:**
  - all four assignments and all five responses;
  - keyboard: Enter on a focused option selects it, and Tab then Space selects the next, with a visible focus outline;
  - a tap at 375px with mobile emulation;
  - the state survives a reload;
  - no overflow at 375px.
