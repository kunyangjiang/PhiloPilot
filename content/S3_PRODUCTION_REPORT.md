## 8. Component (`ui/media/s3/CausationCases.tsx`)

- **Placement:** in 三, right after the paragraph under the table of six cases (anchor: a paragraph in 三 starting "表格是本章的整理，" and containing "下面的组件把这些案例").
- **What it does:** six cases (one assassin, early pre-emption, late pre-emption, firing squad, barometer, oxygen). The reader gives a verdict (是原因 / 不是原因 / 拿不准). The component then shows what four analyses say: the simple regularity theory, simple counterfactual dependence, Lewis's chains, and structural equations / intervention. When all six are done, it counts agreement with the reader's firm verdicts, and adds the chapter's caution that agreement is not truth. Nothing is graded.
- **Uncertainty preserved:**
  - The counterfactual and chain verdicts, and the regularity verdict on the barometer, restate the chapter's table.
  - Every other regularity verdict and all interventionist verdicts are the component's application of the chapter's definitions. Each is marked "本组件按本章的定义推出的判断".
  - The component notes that structural-equation verdicts depend on how the model is built.
  - The chapter's "intuition" column is not presented as the answer.
- **Saved state:** `philopilot.v2.s3.media.causation-cases`, with a 重新开始 button. Verified to survive a reload, and verified not to change the reading position or progress.
- **Input and accessibility:**
  - native buttons with `aria-pressed` in labelled groups;
  - a 343px-wide layout at 375px, with no horizontal overflow;
  - no animation or transition.
