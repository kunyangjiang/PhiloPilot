# PhiloPilot V17 — 体系、马克思主义与当代中国哲学 (CN8) image approval sheet

> Prepared 2026-11-08 for the Product Owner. **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded.** The course is fully integrated without images; no placeholders exist. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold).

Metadata was read from the Wikimedia Commons API on 2026-11-08; direct URLs are derived from the exact filename. "PD" means public domain.

**Why this sheet is short.** The brief asks for historical publications and documents before portraits. For this course none were found on Commons with usable provenance: no scan of 《新唯识论》 (1932/1944), 《论道》 (1940), the 1958 《民主评论》 issue, or the first printings of 《实践论》《矛盾论》. Most later photographs of 熊十力, 唐君毅 and 牟宗三 are recent enough that their PD claims need checking. Only two candidates are recommended; three are listed as conditional.
