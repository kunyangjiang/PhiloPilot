## 5. Comparisons

Two six-question comparisons, each labelled （本章的比较） and each with a "where the analogy fails" row:

1. **Sāṃkhya dualism and Cartesian dualism (六).** Sāṃkhya puts thinking (buddhi) on the side of matter and consciousness (puruṣa) on the other. The line falls in a different place from Descartes'.
2. **The flying man, Descartes and zombies (六).** Each imagines something different: self-awareness without the body, mind without extension, and a body without experience.

The component (四) also sets the three thought experiments side by side. It states that each rests on a different inference.

## 8. Component (`ui/media/s5/ThoughtExperimentMap.tsx`)

- **Placement:** in 四, right after the paragraph under the table of the three thought experiments (anchor: the paragraph starting "表格是本章的整理。下面的组件请你逐一检查").
- **What it does:** the reader picks one thought experiment (Mary's room, zombies, or the bat) and marks each of its three premises as accepted, rejected or unsure.
  - **Rejecting a premise** shows the position or reply the chapter links to that move.
  - **Once every premise is answered:**
    - accepting all three shows the conclusion;
    - rejecting any one says the conclusion need not be accepted, at a cost;
    - leaving one unsure leaves the conclusion open.
  - **"What it does not establish":** a list shown in every case.
  - **When all three experiments are done:** a summary of the reader's choices, plus the chapter's point that the three rest on different inferences.
  - **Grading:** nothing is graded.
- **Uncertainty preserved:**
  - The bat's conclusion is stated as weaker than the other two: Nagel does not argue that physicalism is false.
  - For zombie premise 3, the component says the chapter discusses no position that rejects it, rather than inventing one.
  - For bat premises 1 and 2, linking the premise to the reply is the component's own arrangement, and it is marked as such in the component and in its limits panel.
- **Correction this cycle:** a doubled full stop after each conclusion was removed.
- **Saved state:** `philopilot.v2.s5.media.thought-experiment-map`, with a 重新开始 button. Verified to survive a reload and not to change the reading position.
- **Input and accessibility:**
  - `Pick` button groups with labels; the label of each premise group includes the premise text;
  - `aria-live` on the revealed replies and conclusions;
  - checked with mouse, keyboard and emulated touch;
  - no overflow at 375px (component width 343px);
  - no animation.
