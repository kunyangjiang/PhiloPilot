# PhiloPilot — 黑格尔之后：马克思、克尔凯郭尔、叔本华与尼采 (EA6) image approval sheet

> Prepared 2026-11-10 for the Product Owner (V24). **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded, and there are no placeholders.** The course is fully integrated without images. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold). After approval, each file would be downloaded once, inspected, recorded in `prototype/navigation-v2/public/media/ea6/SOURCES.md` and credited in its caption. **V24 does not authorize any download**; a download needs a separate go-ahead.

Rights are given **as the source states them**. This cycle did not read the rights field on any item page, so every licence below is "not recorded". The age of a text (1841, 1844, 1846, 1867, 1887) is **not** taken as evidence of the status of an edition or its scan.

All candidates are printed editions whose text layers the chapter checked in this cycle.
