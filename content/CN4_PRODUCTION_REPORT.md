## 2. Arguments

The course has one through-line: **where does order come from: rules, rulers, cosmos, or careful reasoning about all three?** Each part puts the main argument in a form the learner can check against the quoted text.

**Part I: state formation.**
- *Classification argument.* "法家" is a bibliographic and retrospective category, not a school with a lineage. The evidence: 司马谈's six-school scheme, and the 艺文志 listing 晁错 beside 商君 and 韩子.
- *更法.* "治世不一道" uses the opponent's own authority (the ancient kings differed among themselves) to defeat appeals to antiquity. Its limit: it tears down the old case without building a positive case for the new law.
- *以刑去刑* (heavy punishment deters). Han Fei's 丽水金 story is used as an internal objection: certainty matters more than severity ("不必得也").
- *The 法 / 术 / 势 split.* Shown as three fragmentary emphases (商鞅, 申不害, 慎到), later combined by Han Fei.

**Part II: Han Fei.**
- *Starting point.* 法 / 术 / 势 is reconstructed as a response to a principal–agent problem. The ruler lacks information, ministers have their own interests (备内), and so the ruler needs public standards (法), private verification (术, 形名), and positional power that does not depend on virtue (势).
- *"其言小而功大者亦罰".* Explained as protecting the accuracy of reports.
- *The 中人 argument in 难势.* Set out as premises → conclusion, with three objections.
- *"法家法治 = rule of law?"* The answer is no. A comparison table shows the differences: the ruler is above the law, there is no independent judiciary, and law is an instrument of the ruler. The section follows SEP's account of the 法治 / 人治 framing (梁启超).
- *Who constrains the ruler?* Presented as the system's main gap, and linked to S9 and D5.

**Part III: Qin–Han synthesis.**
- *Methodological argument.* Separate doctrine, rhetoric, institutions and retrospective narrative before asserting "Confucianism replaced Legalism".
- *Evidence.* Qin already fused law with 五德 cosmology ("合五德之數"). 焚书 was a policy with exceptions, and the two 史记 accounts differ. Shuihudi shows routine administration.
- *Dong Zhongshu* is built only on the 对策 in 汉书, with the authorship problems of 春秋繁露 explained. "罢黜百家，独尊儒术" is shown to be a later formula, not Dong's words. 元帝纪 "汉家自有制度…霸王道雜之" is used as evidence of a mixed system.
- *灾异.* Presented as a two-edged right of speech that could constrain or endanger (辽东高庙).

**Part IV: correlative cosmology.**
- *Five Phases.* Processes, not stuff.
- *Correspondence tables.* Differences between tables (the sacrificial organs in 時則訓 vs the medical mapping) show they are conventions, not discoveries.
- *同类相动.* Reconstructed in steps. The diagnosis is that "类" shifts from a physical sense (resonating strings) to a value sense (good rule ↔ auspicious omen).
- *天文训's list.* Mixes real causation (burning mirror), misattributed real phenomena (dew), and fantasy. The lesson is that the method lacked a way to separate them.
- *夷伯庙.* Read three ways (刘向 / 董仲舒 / 刘歆), showing that the system underdetermines interpretation.
- *Charitable reading.* Four reasons for linking politics and cosmos, then an evaluation table.

**Part V: Yijing.**
- *Layers first.* Classic / commentaries / Han interpretation / later philosophy.
- *元亨利贞.* A single phrase read three ways shows layer-dependence.
- *Divination procedure.* Taught as procedure plus probability, to show what the method actually is. There is an explicit statement that no reliable evidence shows divination predicts the future.
- *Why a classic?* The philosophical value of 系辞 is argued to be independent of predictive validity.

**Part VI: Wang Chong.**
- *Methods.* 效验 (testing against evidence), 问难 (cross-examining authorities), 自然 (no intentional Heaven).
- *Coincidence.* "遭""适""偶" is his key move against correlation-as-causation.
- *Limits.* 骨相 and 治期 fatalism ("世之治亂，在時不在政"), plus the 宣汉 / 验符 acceptance of auspicious omens. Three interpretations are given; none is forced.
- *Not a modern scientist.* No controlled experiment, though the 水灌冶鑄 analogy for thunder is a striking empirical model.

**批评与回应** (optional):
1. Qin's fall disproves Legalism (盐铁论 非鞅 debate).
2. "Legalism = totalitarianism" (label assessed and partly rejected).
3. Was the Confucian–Legalist opposition real? (盐铁论 本议 vs 八经).
4. Did Wang Chong remove the only constraint on rulers?

## 3. Texts and verification

**Method.** Every classical quotation in the manuscript was checked character-by-character against a digital edition. Wikisource (zh) was fetched via its API into the scratchpad; ctext.org was not scraped. A script (`verify.py`, scratchpad only) extracted every blockquote and every inline quote containing traditional characters, normalised punctuation, split on "……", and searched the cached source texts. It also checked that footnote numbering is sequential. The final run reports all quotes found, with three intentional exceptions (below).

**Flagged emendations** (the base text has an editorial correction; the manuscript quotes the corrected reading and explains it):
1. 《汉书·元帝纪》 "純住德教": the manuscript reads 住 → **任**, following the standard emendation, and says so in a note.
2. 《论衡·问孔》 "（迢）〔追〕": quoted as 追, with the edition's bracket convention explained.
3. 《论衡》 on 京房 "《易》京氏布六十（四）卦": the edition deletes 四. The manuscript quotes 六十卦 and explains the deletion inline (Part V, 「为什么《易》会成为哲学经典？」).

**Deliberately not quoted:**
- Shuihudi, Mawangdui and other excavated texts are described, not quoted, because no verified transcription was available in the sources used.
- 《荀子》 pages failed to load, so the planned 荀子 quote was replaced by a paraphrase with a pointer to the Confucius, Mencius and Xunzi course.
- No modern translation is quoted. Modern scholarship (Graham, Graziani, Pines, Shaughnessy, Loewe, Queen, Arbuckle, Pokora) is summarised in the course's own words from SEP, IEP and Wikipedia overviews. Each such note states "本课没有阅读" (the course has not read the work itself). The SEP entries on Legalism and Han Fei were consulted, and the Part II wording on 法治 and the entrapped sovereign was revised to match them.

**Modern-Chinese explanation.** Every blockquote is followed by a plain modern-Chinese explanation, not a word-for-word translation. Key terms (法, 术, 势, 形名, 类, 感应, 之卦, 效验, 自然) are glossed on first use.

## 4. Editorial decisions

1. **"法家" is a label.** It is introduced from the start as a Han classification. The course speaks of "被归为法家的思想家" and shows the disagreements among them (商鞅 vs 韩非 on severity; 申不害 / 慎到 as fragments).
2. **Dong Zhongshu on reliable evidence only.** His views are built from the 汉书 对策. Material from 春秋繁露 is quoted as "题为董仲舒所作" and placed in Part IV as part of Han correlative thought, not as Dong's own doctrine. "罢黜百家，独尊儒术" is treated as a retrospective slogan.
3. **No equation of 法 with the rule of law.** A table compares the two, and the Part II text follows SEP's framing. The "entrapped sovereign" debate is presented as a scholarly reading, not a settled fact.
4. **The four-layer method in Part III.** Separating doctrine, rhetoric, institutions and retrospective narrative replaces the "Legalist Qin → Confucian Han" story. This is the part's main teaching device.
5. **Yijing without fortune-telling.**
   - The divination procedure is taught because one cannot understand the text without it.
   - The worked example uses an invented question ("要不要换一个工作").
   - The course states that there is no reliable evidence of predictive power.
   - Interpretive rules for changing lines are presented as varied, not authoritative.
   - Practice items never ask learners to interpret a reading for themselves.
6. **Correlative cosmology read charitably first.** It is presented as an organising framework, with four reasons it was attractive, before it is evaluated. The critique targets a specific inferential step (the shift in "类"), not the whole worldview.
7. **Wang Chong is not a hero of science.** His limits (fatalism, physiognomy, omens favourable to the dynasty) get their own section, with three interpretations offered without a verdict.
8. **The 批评与回应 section** is optional, and placed after Part VI so the main sequence stays continuous. Two critiques return to the power-constraint question from Part II.
9. **Course names, not codes, in learner-facing text.** Links point to 《政治哲学：权威、正义与权利》, 《跨传统的政治秩序与正当性》, 《孔子、孟子与荀子：修养、礼与人性》, 《道家传统：老子、庄子及其后世解释》, 《亚里士多德：实体、原因、逻辑与好的生活》 and 《魏晋玄学与中国佛教：空、性与顿悟》.
10. **Length.** About 51.7k CJK characters, above recent courses, because the manuscript includes primary texts with explanations. If length must be reduced, the first candidates are:
    - the 慎到 section;
    - the 京房 卦气 paragraph;
    - two of the six 王充 critiques (变动, 物势);
    - the 批评与回应 section.

## 5. Media plan

Option A (a Legalism timeline / map) is **not** recommended as a separate component. The chronology is short, and the four-layer table in Part III already does that work. Options **B, C, D and E** are recommended, in priority order D > C > B > E.

### D. Hexagram Explorer (卦象探索器)

- **Location.** Part V, after 「一个完整的例子：泰之临」.
- **Objective.** Let learners build a hexagram line by line from 6/7/8/9 values, see the resulting 本卦 and 之卦, and understand the procedure and probabilities. It must not provide readings.
- **Visual design.**
  - Six stacked horizontal bars drawn bottom-up in SVG: solid for yang, broken for yin. Changing lines carry a small ○ (9) or × (6) marker.
  - The lower and upper trigrams are bracketed and labelled with name, image and 说卦 attribute.
  - The 之卦 is drawn alongside with an arrow.
  - Below the figure is a small probability strip comparing yarrow (1/16, 5/16, 7/16, 3/16) with coins (1/8, 3/8, 3/8, 1/8).
- **User actions.**
  - Tap each line to cycle through 6 → 7 → 8 → 9.
  - "Random (yarrow odds)" and "Random (coin odds)" buttons.
  - A "Show 泰之临 example" preset.
  - Toggle the 之卦 view.
- **Feedback.**
  - Hexagram name and number, trigram names, and which lines change.
  - The judgment text of the 本卦 from the classic, with no interpretation.
  - A fixed note: "本工具只演示卦的构成和概率，不提供占断。"
  - After 50 or more random draws, a running tally of line values next to the expected odds.
- **Limitations.**
  - No interpretation and no free-text question field.
  - Only the 卦辞 of the 64 hexagrams; no 爻辞 beyond the two worked examples.
  - Changing-line rules are not applied, because the traditions differ.
- **Mobile / desktop.**
  - Mobile: a vertical stack, with 44 px tap targets per line and the 之卦 below the 本卦.
  - Desktop: 本卦 and 之卦 side by side, with the probability strip to the right.
- **Minimal implementation.**
  - One React component holding `lines: (6|7|8|9)[]` state.
  - A static `HEXAGRAMS` table of 64 entries (binary key → name, 卦辞), plus an `EIGHT_TRIGRAMS` table.
  - Pure SVG with no library; a seeded RNG for the random buttons.
  - About 250 lines of code plus about 6 KB of data.

### C. Five Phases Correspondence Explorer (五行对应表)

- **Location.** Part IV, after 「一张对应表」.
- **Objective.** Show 相生 and 相胜 as two cycles over the same five phases. Let learners see that correspondence rows are conventional by comparing two sources that disagree (organs in 時則訓 vs the medical mapping).
- **Visual design.**
  - Five nodes on a pentagon (木, 火, 土, 金, 水), coloured with muted category colours that also carry text labels.
  - 相生 is drawn as outer arrows and 相胜 as an inner star; each can be toggled.
  - A side table lists the rows for the selected phase: season, direction, colour, taste, organ, emotion.
- **User actions.**
  - Tap a phase to highlight its row.
  - Toggle 相生 / 相胜.
  - A "Compare sources" switch shows the organ row from 時則訓 vs the medical tradition, with the differences highlighted.
- **Feedback.**
  - Each row cites its source text.
  - When the sources disagree, a callout explains that correspondence tables differed between texts.
- **Limitations.**
  - Only rows attested in the texts quoted in the course; no invented rows.
  - No "personal element" or health advice.
- **Mobile / desktop.**
  - Mobile: pentagon on top and the table as a collapsible list below.
  - Desktop: pentagon and table side by side.
- **Minimal implementation.**
  - Static data object `{ phase, rows: { season, direction, … , organ: { shize, medical } } }`.
  - SVG pentagon with computed coordinates.
  - Two path layers with CSS opacity toggles.
  - About 180 lines of code.

### B. Names and Performance Workshop (形名参验)

- **Location.** Part II, after 「典冠的故事」.
- **Objective.** Make the logic of 二柄 concrete: the ruler rewards or punishes on the match between a minister's claim (名) and the result (形), not on how large the result is.
- **Visual design.**
  - Four case cards, each with a minister's claimed task and an outcome bar. The cases are: as promised; over-delivered; under-delivered; and "典冠", where someone acts outside their office.
  - Each card has a reward / punish choice.
- **User actions.** Choose reward or punish for each card, then press "按韩非的标准核对" ("check by Han Fei's standard").
- **Feedback.**
  - Each card shows Han Fei's verdict with the 二柄 sentence, including "其言小而功大者亦罰".
  - Then a short note asks: "这个标准在现代组织中有什么代价？"
- **Limitations.**
  - This is not a practice item. It is ungraded except for showing Han Fei's own rule.
  - The note explicitly marks the company analogy's limits (death penalty, no accountability of the ruler).
- **Mobile / desktop.**
  - Mobile: cards stack vertically.
  - Desktop: a 2 × 2 grid.
- **Minimal implementation.** React state for four choices plus static case data. No SVG needed beyond simple bars. About 120 lines of code.

### E. Wang Chong Evidence Workshop (效验工作台)

- **Location.** Part VI, after 「批评六：卜筮不问天地」.
- **Objective.** Simulate 王充's coincidence argument: if rewards / punishments and warm / cold weather are independent, how often do "matches" appear anyway?
- **Visual design.**
  - A 12-month grid. Each cell shows the weather (warm or cold) and the ruler's act (reward or punish).
  - Matches (reward + warm, punish + cold) are highlighted.
  - A counter compares the observed matches with the 50% expected by chance.
- **User actions.** "Generate a random year", "Generate 100 years", and a "Show only matching months" toggle, which simulates selective reporting by an omen interpreter.
- **Feedback.**
  - Match rate per year and a histogram over 100 years.
  - When "only matching months" is on, the display says: this is what a 变复之家 would record.
  - The 寒温 sentence "遭與賞罰同時" is shown.
- **Limitations.**
  - A simplification, with two states only. It illustrates selection bias, not historical data.
  - States plainly that this is a modern illustration, not Wang Chong's own method.
- **Mobile / desktop.**
  - Mobile: a 3 × 4 grid with the histogram below.
  - Desktop: a 6 × 2 grid with the histogram on the right.
- **Minimal implementation.**
  - React state with a seeded RNG.
  - The histogram as plain SVG rects.
  - About 150 lines of code.

**Shared constraints for all components:**
- No network calls or external libraries.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- All information is conveyed in text as well as colour.
- Keyboard-operable controls.
- Each component degrades to the static table already in the manuscript.

## 8. Uncertainties and open points

1. **春秋繁露 authorship.** The manuscript takes a cautious, mainstream position: attributed, partly compiled, the Five Phases chapters probably later. Specialists disagree on particular chapters. The course never relies on 春秋繁露 for a claim about Dong Zhongshu himself.
2. **The 对策 text.** Some scholars suspect Ban Gu edited it. This is mentioned, and the manuscript calls it only "relatively more reliable".
3. **Emendations.** The three flagged emendations (§3) follow standard editions. A specialist reader may prefer to quote the base text with brackets.
4. **Excavated texts.** Shuihudi, Zhangjiashan, the Shanghai Museum 周易 and the Mawangdui 周易 are described from Wikipedia overviews, without quotation. Dates and contents are given at a general level only; a specialist should check them before release.
5. **Correspondence tables.** Rows were drawn from 時則訓. The medical mapping and the similarity to 月令 / 吕氏春秋 are stated as general knowledge. Note 13 says these were not checked against 《礼记》, 《吕氏春秋》 or 《黄帝内经》.
6. **The 夷伯庙 readings.** They follow the 五行志 text as fetched. Attribution of the second reading to 董仲舒 rests on 五行志's own label.
7. **Yarrow probabilities.** These are the standard calculation for the 大衍 procedure as usually reconstructed from 系辞 (later formalised by 朱熹). The manuscript does not discuss alternative reconstructions; a specialist may want a one-line caveat.
8. **Modern scholarship.** It is summarised only through SEP, IEP and Wikipedia overviews, with "本课没有阅读" in each note. Page numbers appear only where SEP gives them (Graham 1989, pp. 290–292). They should be spot-checked if page references are wanted.
9. **Practice option order.** All `single` answers are `a`. Shuffle before release.
10. **Length.** The manuscript is long (§4.10). Possible cuts are listed there, but nothing has been cut.
11. **Wikisource text variants.** Wikisource editions are not critical editions. Character-level variants (for example 敎 / 教, 蓺 / 藝) follow the fetched text. A final pass against a printed edition (中华书局 点校本 for 史记 / 汉书; 黄晖《论衡校释》; 王先慎《韩非子集解》) is recommended if budget allows.
