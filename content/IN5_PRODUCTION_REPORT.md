## 6. Interactive teaching proposals (not built)

These proposals follow the same rules as IN4:
- only passages already verified in the manuscript;
- a visible source tier on every panel (原典 / 注释 / 论敌原文 / 二手转述 / 本章重建);
- no new claims;
- Swiss UI tokens;
- the manuscript's existing table as the no-JavaScript fallback.

None of the five candidates simulates a ritual or its supposed effect. The sacrifice appears only as a sentence to be analysed, never as an animation of a cause producing heaven or cattle.

**Recommended set: A, C and D.** These cover the three technical cores of the course: the injunction (Part 一), sentence meaning (Part 三) and svataḥprāmāṇya (Part 四). They are also the places where a static table is weakest, because the learner needs to *manipulate* a sentence or a cognition. B and E are good optional additions. B overlaps with A and D, and E partly overlaps with IN3's testimony material.

### A. 吠陀命令句解释工作坊 (Vedic Injunction Interpretation Workshop) — recommended

- **Location:** Part 一. The main anchor is "### 一个例子：命令的结构" (table "成分 | 在"想要天界的人应当举行火祭"中 | 弥曼差派怎样理解"). The workshop continues into "### 义务与后果" (table "问题 | 一种后果主义的读法 | 弥曼差派的复杂之处") and "### 有害的祭祀也是"法"吗？" (the śyena table).
- **Objective:**
  - see the three components of an injunction: eligible agent, act, and injunctive form;
  - see why the śyena case shows that the eligibility clause states a condition, not a command;
  - see why the Mīmāṃsā view does not reduce to "do it for the result".
- **Visual form:** three panels.
  1. **The sentence.** It is shown as three segments in token colours: 资格者 | 行为 | 命令的形式. A toggle switches between two sentences: "想要天界的人应当举行火祭" and "行咒害的人应当用鹰祭祭祀".
  2. **"是不是法？"** A checklist with two boxes: 被命令 and 有益.
  3. **"为什么应当做？"** Two columns: a consequentialist reading and the Mīmāṃsā complication, filled row by row from the manuscript's table.
- **Learner action:**
  1. Tap each segment to see its role.
  2. For the śyena sentence, decide whether each checklist box applies, then reveal Śabara's answer.
  3. In panel 3, predict the Mīmāṃsā answer to "我们怎样知道它会带来天界？" before it is revealed.
- **Philosophical feedback:**
  - the śyena reveal quotes Śabara on 1.1.2 ("śyenenābhicaran yajeteti hi samāmananti, nābhicaritavyam iti") with its 本章试译;
  - ticking "被命令（伤害）" explains that the command concerns the means, given the condition, and not the harm;
  - panel 3 ends on Śālikanātha's *kārya* sentence and on the manuscript's point that the consequentialist/deontological split does not map directly onto Mīmāṃsā (Clooney via SEP).
- **Historical qualifications:**
  - the vidhi / arthavāda / mantra classification shown is Śabara's (on 1.4.1), and the fuller classifications of later manuals are not shown;
  - the agnihotra sentence is given as Śabara quotes it, without checking its Vedic source;
  - "hypothetical imperative" is labelled as a modern term;
  - the category of daily, unconditioned obligation is mentioned but not modelled, as in the manuscript.
- **Desktop / mobile:** on desktop, the three panels sit left to right. On mobile they stack, and the segments wrap.
- **Minimal approach:**


### B. 无作者性与证言论证地图 (Authorlessness and Testimony Argument Map) — optional

  - see the whole authorlessness argument as a map;
  - locate where each opponent attacks it:
    - Vātsyāyana attacks the inference from eternality to validity;
    - Dharmakīrti attacks it twice: symmetry of virtues and faults, and convention;
    - Udayana's theist inference is the opposite conclusion from the same premise ("a meaningful sentence needs a speaker").
- **Visual form:** an SVG node-and-edge map.
  - The central chain: 词义关系本来就有 → 吠陀没有作者 → 没有作者的过失 → 吠陀不会因作者而出错.

  1. Drag each opponent node, or tap it and then tap an edge, to the edge it attacks.
  2. Then answer: "哪一个攻击针对的是前提，哪一个针对的是推理？".
- **Philosophical feedback:**
  - correct placements reveal the quoted verse or sentence with its 本章试译;
  - wrong placements explain what the opponent actually concedes (for example, Nyāya *agrees* the Veda is valid);
  - the final card states the manuscript's summary that the real disagreement is whether "having no speaker" is a merit or an impossibility.
- **Historical qualifications:**
  - Kumārila's own anti-God verses were not located in the files read, and the node says so;
  - the documented-exchange / reconstruction distinction from Part 六 is shown as edge style: solid for documented exchanges, dotted for reconstructions.
- **Desktop / mobile:** on desktop, a full map. On mobile, the chain becomes a vertical list, and each step opens a sheet listing its attackers; no dragging is needed.

### D. 认识的自身有效性工作坊 (Self-Validity of Cognition Workshop) — recommended

- **Location:** Part 四. Anchors:
  - "### 鸠摩利罗的主张与论证" (six-step reconstruction);
  - "### 有效性与可错性" (table "推翻的方式 | 梵文 | 例子");
  - "### 普拉巴卡拉派：一切经验都有效，错误是没有分辨" (four-row comparison table);
  - "### 和正理派、佛教的比较".
- **Objective:**
  - see why "verify before trusting" leads to regress;
  - see how the Bhāṭṭa defeater model works instead;
  - see the more radical Prābhākara account: every experience is valid, and error is a failure to discriminate;
  - see that "valid" means "entitled until defeated", not "true".
- **Visual form:** three modes on one SVG canvas.
  1. **后退:** a chain C1 ← C2 ← C3 … that grows as the learner presses "证明它", and never closes.
  2. **推翻（巴陀派）:** a single node, "那里有银子", with a solid border. Two defeater buttons ("走近一看：是贝壳" / "知道眼睛有病") turn the border dashed and add Pārthasārathi's label for the defeater type.
  3. **未分辨（普拉巴卡拉派）:** the jaundiced-eye case. The single node "黄色的海螺" splits into two solid nodes ("眼中的黄色" and "海螺") joined by a red "未分辨" link. The link, not either node, is what fails at the action stage.
