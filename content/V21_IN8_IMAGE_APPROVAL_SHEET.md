> Prepared 2026-11-09 for the Product Owner (V21). **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded, and there are no placeholders.** The course is fully integrated without images. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold). After approval, each file would be downloaded once, inspected, recorded in `public/media/in8/SOURCES.md` and credited in its caption. **V21 does not authorize any download**, even for approved items; a download needs a separate go-ahead.

Metadata was read on 2026-11-09 from the Wikimedia Commons API (`prop=imageinfo`, `extmetadata`). Rights are given **as Commons states them**; this sheet does not claim any use is legally cleared. Several Commons "public domain" tags for Indian photographs rest on Indian term rules (anonymous works, 60 years from publication); US status was not investigated (no new licensing investigation in V21).
