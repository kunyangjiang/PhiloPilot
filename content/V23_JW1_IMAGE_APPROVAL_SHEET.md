# PhiloPilot — 犹太哲学：从萨阿迪亚到列维纳斯 (JW1) image approval sheet

> Prepared 2026-11-10 for the Product Owner (V23). **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded, and there are no placeholders.** The course is fully integrated without images. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold). After approval, each file would be downloaded once, inspected, recorded in `prototype/navigation-v2/public/media/jw1/SOURCES.md` and credited in its caption. **V23 does not authorize any download**; a download needs a separate go-ahead.

Rights are given **as the source states them**. This cycle did not read the rights field on any item page, so every licence below is "not recorded". The age of the philosophical text is **not** taken as evidence of the status of a scan or of a printed edition. No broad rights investigation was opened.

All candidates are printed editions whose pages the chapter already read in this cycle to check its quotations. They show the reader where each quoted line comes from. No portrait from life exists for Saadia, Maimonides, Gersonides or Crescas; later imagined portraits are not proposed.
