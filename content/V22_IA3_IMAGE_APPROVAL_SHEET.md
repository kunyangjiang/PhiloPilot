# PhiloPilot — 安萨里、阿威罗伊与西方伊斯兰思想 (IA3) image approval sheet

> Prepared 2026-11-09 for the Product Owner (V22). **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded, and there are no placeholders.** The course is fully integrated without images. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold). After approval, each file would be downloaded once, inspected, recorded in `prototype/navigation-v2/public/media/ia3/SOURCES.md` and credited in its caption. **V22 does not authorize any download**; a download needs a separate go-ahead.

Rights are given **as the source states them**, per file. Where this cycle did not read a rights statement on the item page, the sheet says so; the age of a text is not taken as evidence of its status. No broad rights investigation was opened.

All candidates are printed editions whose page images the chapter already read on 2026-11-09 to transcribe its Arabic (kept in the session's temporary folder, not in the project). They show the reader where each quoted line comes from. There is no portrait of al-Ghazālī, Averroes, Ibn Ṭufayl or Ibn Khaldūn from life; later imagined portraits, statues and stamps are not proposed.
