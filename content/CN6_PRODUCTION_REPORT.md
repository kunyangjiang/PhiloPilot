## 4. Terminology decisions

- **Seven tracked terms** (理 / 气 / 心 / 性 / 知 / 行 / 欲) are in a table in the 导读. The table names the 欲 reversal (理学 opposition → 戴震 "理是欲的分寸") as a thread through the course.
- **"理学 / 道学 / 宋明理学 / Neo-Confucianism"**: 道学 is introduced from 宋史 as the self-description, and the others as later umbrella terms.
- **"程朱 / 陆王"** are used as conventional labels. The 导读 and Part 四 reject "客观唯心主义 / 主观唯心主义" and replace them with a comparison table.
- **"阳儒阴释"** is named only to be split into the three relations (documented response / contact / comparison).
- **格物**: 朱熹 "至 / 盡" vs 王阳明 "正". 物 is "事" in 王阳明 (意之所在).
- **知行合一**: never glossed as 言行一致. Akrasia is introduced as 意志软弱.
- **"规范性的话语"**, "关于实际活动的证据" and "女性自己的声音" are fixed terms in Part 七.
- **Course cross-references** use registry course names only.

## 5. Critiques & Responses decision

**Included**, decided independently of CN5 and with different critiques:
1. "理学 = 礼教 = 以理杀人": the strongest external critique, and the one learners most often meet. Answered by separating doctrine, institutional use and later polemic, with the open question kept open.
2. "空谈心性误国": answered with the officials' records and the 清谈 parallel, and passed on to the course on Chinese philosophy and modernity.
3. "Reading 理学 as 'philosophy' strips out its practice": a methodological self-critique, which matters because Part 五 relies on separating 立言宗旨 from claims.

The 阳儒阴释 critique is handled in the 导读 and in Part 一 rather than here, to avoid duplication.

## 6. Media proposals

Five components are proposed. Recommended priority is **E > C > A**; **D and B are optional.** None is implemented. The shared constraints are the same as CN5 §6: no network or libraries, reduced motion, text plus colour, keyboard access, and a static fallback.

### A. 概念旅行图 (shared component with CN5)

- **Location.** 导读 term table; again at the Part 六 summary.
- **Objective.** The same component as CN5-A, with CN6 data for 理 / 气 / 心 / 性 / 知 / 行 / 欲. Its key feature is a **cross-course link layer**: the 性 row continues from CN5 (佛性 / 见性) into CN6 (性即理 → 性日生).
- **Safeguards.** Every arrow between cells and between courses is typed as documented response / documented contact / comparison, matching §11. No arrow may be untyped.
- **Implementation.** It reuses the CN5 component and adds a `course` field and a `crossLinks[]` array. About 40 extra lines.

### B. 气的聚散 (Qi gathering and dispersing)

- **Location.** Part 二, after 「张载：太虚即气」.
- **Objective.** Make 张载's 冰 / 水 analogy and his dilemma visual.
- **Visual form.** About 60 dots in a field. "聚" draws them into a shape labelled 客形; "散" lets them drift back. A counter always shows 60, illustrating 无无: nothing is lost.
- **Interaction.** Gather and disperse buttons. A three-way model switch: **太虚即气** (counter constant), **虚能生气** (dots appear from an empty "虚" box; the 老氏 critique is shown), **万象为所见** (dots vanish when the "eye" toggle is off; the 浮屠 critique is shown).
- **Feedback.** The relevant 正蒙 sentence for each model.
- **Safeguards.** A fixed note: "这是本课的示意，不是张载的物理学". No claim about modern physics.
- **Mobile / desktop.** Mobile: field on top, quote below. Desktop: side by side. With reduced motion, the transitions are instant.
- **Minimal implementation.** Seeded positions, CSS transforms, three models. About 160 lines.

### C. 孝之理在哪里？ (Where is the principle of filial piety?)

- **Location.** Part 四, after 「心即理：一个论证」.
- **Objective.** Let learners run 王阳明's "亲没之后" thought experiment and see where it works and where it does not.
- **Visual form.** Four cards: 孝 (father alive), 孝 (father deceased), 竹子空心, 礼器尺寸. Each card has two buttons: "理在对象" / "理在心".
- **Interaction.** The learner places each card. Then "看朱熹怎么说" and "看王阳明怎么说" overlays show the respective position with a quotation.
- **Feedback.**
  - For the deceased-father card, the 传习录 卷中 sentence.
  - For 名物度数, 王阳明's own concession.
  - A closing line: "这个论证对道德之理最有力".
- **Safeguards.** Ungraded. Both thinkers' positions are shown with equal space. No "winner" label.
- **Mobile / desktop.** Mobile: one card at a time. Desktop: a 2 × 2 grid.
- **Minimal implementation.** Static case data with quotes. About 120 lines.

### D. 知行合一的三种读法 (Three readings of the unity of knowing and acting)

- **Location.** Part 五, before the summary table.
- **Objective.** Sort six quotations into strong / cultivational / weak readings.
- **Interaction.** Tap a quotation, then tap a column. Afterwards, "看本课的分法" reveals the manuscript's assignment and lets the learner keep their own.
- **Safeguards.** It is presented as the course's classification, and disagreement is allowed. It overlaps practice set 五, so it is **optional**.
- **Implementation.** About 100 lines.

### E. 证据分类板 (Evidence sorting board: norms vs practice)

- **Location.** Part 七, before 「小结：规范与实际」.
- **Objective.** The core method skill of Part 七: tell prescriptive discourse from evidence of activity and from women's own voices.
- **Visual form.** Eight source cards (女诫 卑弱; 女诫 序; 程颐 寡妇; 陆氏 识字; 陆氏 色难; 四库 提要; 李贽; 随园 1790) and three columns. A fourth column holds "关于女性的争论".
- **Interaction.** Drag, or tap to place. "核对" shows the manuscript's placement with a one-line reason. A second step asks, for each card, "它不能告诉我们什么？"
- **Feedback.** It explains cases with dual placement (陆氏 is both a woman's voice and prescriptive). It is ungraded.
- **Safeguards.** Only quotations already in the manuscript. No generalisation to "Chinese women" in any feedback line. Each card shows its source's class background where the manuscript gives it.
- **Mobile / desktop.** Mobile: tap-to-place with stacked columns. Desktop: drag between four columns.
- **Minimal implementation.** Static card data `{id, quote, cite, placement[], cannotTell}`. About 150 lines.

## 9. Academic uncertainties

1. **二程 attribution.** Many 遗书 entries are unmarked. Only entries marked 明 / 正, or from 程颐-specific 卷 (18, 21, 22, 25), are attributed (note 3).
2. **定性书.** It is quoted from 近思录, not from 二程文集. Its relation to Buddhist and 玄学 discussions is presented as comparison (note 5).
3. **The 太极图说 first line** and the 无极 debate. The letters were not read (note 7).
4. **朱熹 and Chan / Huayan.** This rests on SEP *Zhu Xi* (author not visible on the page read) and on 戴震's quotation of 答汪尚书 (notes 2, 14).
5. **The examination canon.** The year (1313 decree, 1315 exams) is the common account and was not checked. SEP says "a century later" (note 11).
6. **龙场.** 年谱 is a disciples' compilation; the commonly quoted sentence was **not** used because it could not be verified character by character (note 18).
7. **知行合一 and akrasia.** This follows the SEP *Wang Yangming* entry (Van Norden). Chan 1962 was not read (note 19).
8. **四句教.** Its original meaning, and whether 王畿 or 钱德洪 is closer to it, is open. 王畿's own record, 刘宗周 and the 泰州 school were not read (note 20).
9. **日知录 心学.** The boundary between 黄震 and 顾炎武 (note 22).
10. **戴震's critique.** It may fit later 朱学 better than 朱熹 himself; this is stated as open.
11. **"餓死事極小".** Its later influence and Song widow remarriage are general claims; no social-history study was read (note 27).
12. **Part 七 sources.** All come from elite households. Ko 1994, Mann 1997 and Peterson 2000 were not read; 王贞仪 rests on Wikipedia only (note 32). **A specialist reader in women's history is recommended before release**, because this part is the newest ground for the course series.
13. **章学诚's target.** That it was 袁枚 is the scholarly consensus as generally reported, not checked (note 30).
14. **Length.** About 43,400 CJK characters, comparable with CN5. Part 七 is a full part, as the spec requires; no cuts are proposed.

## 11. CN5 / CN6 boundary and cross-reference note

**Boundary.** CN6 begins where CN5 ends: the Confucian response to a Buddhism whose most developed thinking about 心 and 性 CN5 has already taught. CN6 does **not** re-teach Buddhist doctrine. When it needs 空, 一即一切, 定慧一体 or the 神秀 verse, it names them briefly and points back to the CN5 part.

**Back-references in CN6:**
- Part 一: CN5 六 (心、性); CN5 一 (王弼 应物而无累); CN5 批评二 (清谈).
- Part 二: CN5 一 (以无为本) via 张载's critique.
- Part 三: CN5 四 and 五 (一与多); CN5 六 (镜子, 顿悟).
- Part 五: CN5 六 (定慧一体).
- Part 六: CN5 三 (物不迁) as a comparison with 王夫之.

**Forward boundary.** CN6 ends in the mid-Qing (戴震 1777; 章学诚 and 袁枚 in the 1790s). It explicitly leaves late-Qing thought and the encounter with Western thought to the course on Chinese philosophy and modernity, which has **not** been started.

**Influence vs comparison.** Every CN5 → CN6 link in the manuscript is typed as documented response, documented contact or comparison. The 导读 states the distinction, and Part 一 and Part 五 apply it explicitly. CN6 never calls 理学 a Buddhist derivative.

### Shared CN5 → CN6 concept map

This map is the same in both reports. **Relation** separates three kinds of link:
- **documented response**: a later text explicitly names or answers the earlier position;
- **documented contact**: biography shows the later thinker studied the earlier tradition, but no specific borrowing is shown;
- **comparison**: the course's own juxtaposition, with no evidence of derivation.
