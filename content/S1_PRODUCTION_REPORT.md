## 5. Component

`ui/media/s1/CaseAnalyser.tsx` (`case-analyser`) is placed in 二, after the comparison table of four theories and the paragraph that introduces it.

- **Step 1:** the reader's own verdict on six cases: three "does she know?" cases (十个硬币, 假谷仓县, 法上的沙漠旅行者) and three "is it justified?" cases (千里眼诺曼, 新恶魔的受害者, 被遗忘的证据). Options are 知道 / 不知道 / 拿不准 or 有辩护 / 没有辩护 / 拿不准.
- **Step 2:** after each verdict, what each of the chapter's criteria says about the case and why: JTB, no false lemmas, causal theory, sensitivity, the Nyāya pramāṇa, process reliabilism, internalism.
- **Step 3:** once all six are judged, how often each criterion agrees with the reader's firm verdicts, followed by the chapter's caution that agreement is not truth (Norman and the demon are a pair; one may revise the criterion or the verdict).
- Not scored, and it does not compute a "best theory". Six criterion verdicts that the chapter does not state for that case (for example sensitivity on the ten coins) are derived from the chapter's definitions and marked as such in the interface.

**Justification:** the curriculum's method focus is evaluating theories of justification. The chapter's cases are intuition pumps, so the reader's own verdict has to come first; showing each criterion's verdict afterwards makes the trade-offs visible without settling them.
