## 2. Method

Every claim about exclusion is typed (fact, reason, attribution, influence, quality) and matched to the evidence it needs. Recovery is separated from endorsement (Ban Zhao). Corrections are checked for the reverse error (a positive stereotype replacing a negative one) and for who is still missing (Cooper). The PhiloPilot case states what the site does and does not do, without a verdict on its 12-to-3 distribution between European and African courses.
