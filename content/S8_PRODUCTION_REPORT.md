## 5. Comparisons

One six-question comparison, labelled （本章的比较）, with a "where the analogy fails" row:

- **Mencius' 恻隐之心 and Hume's passions (五).** Both place the source of moral response in feeling rather than in reasoning.
- **Where the analogy fails:**
  - Hume's argument serves an epistemological thesis about the *source* of morality;
  - Mencius' serves a thesis about human *nature* and a programme of cultivation (扩充);
  - Mencius has no reason–passion dichotomy, and Hume has no 扩充.
- **History:** "两者没有历史联系".

Two further juxtapositions (Wang Yangming with Socrates on weakness of will, and with internalism) are marked as constructed for teaching. The chapter's note on comparisons also warns that "realism" and "expressivism" are twentieth-century English-language categories, so placing Mencius or Hume in them is likely to distort.

## 8. Component (`ui/media/s8/MetaethicsMap.tsx`)

- **Placement:** in 四, after the paragraph under the table "四种立场与它们的代价". The anchor is the paragraph starting "表格是本章的整理，每一行都有许多变体".
- **What it does:**
  - **Diagnostic questions.** Up to four questions place the reader:
    1. describing a fact or expressing an attitude;
    2. whether any moral judgement is true;
    3. whether truth comes from attitude-independent facts or from a procedure;
    4. natural or non-natural.

    The answers lead to expressivism, error theory, constructivism, naturalist realism or non-naturalist realism. 拿不准 at any step gives "你的位置还没有确定", which sends the reader back to the relevant section.
  - **Question 5 (motivation).** Asks whether a sincere moral judgement necessarily motivates, then shows how that answer fits the reader's position (for example, internalism plus cognitivism meets Smith's moral problem).
  - **Weighing objections.** The objections the chapter raises against that position are weighed one by one as 我仍然保持 / 动摇了:
    - 3 for each realism;
    - 2 each for error theory, expressivism and constructivism.

    Each objection names the section where it is discussed.
  - **Summary.** A summary follows, and then a course claim: no position answers every question equally well, so choosing one means choosing where to pay.
  - **Grading:** nothing is graded.
- **Uncertainty preserved:**
  - wavering is described as "不等于这个立场是错的";
  - the limits panel says the diagnostic questions simplify each position;
  - variants such as quasi-realism, which say moral judgements are "true", are not listed separately.
- **Saved state:** `philopilot.v2.s8.media.metaethics-map`. Weighings are keyed by position and objection, so changing the answers does not erase another position's weighings. 重新开始 clears everything.
- **Checked in the browser:**
  - all six outcomes (five positions plus "open") were driven through questions 5 and the objections;
  - keyboard: Tab to a choice, then Enter, selects it with a visible 2px focus outline;
  - the state survives a reload;
  - no overflow at 375px.
- **Input and accessibility:**
  - labelled `Pick` groups;
  - `aria-live`;
  - no animation.
