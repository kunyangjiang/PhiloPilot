## 8. Component (`ui/media/s4/IdentityCases.tsx`)

- **Placement:** in 二, right after the paragraph under the table of cases and criteria (anchor: the paragraph starting "表格是本章根据第二部分的介绍整理的").
- **What it does:**
  - six puzzle cases (gradual forgetting / the brave officer, severe dementia, brain transplant, teleportation, duplication, fission) and three criteria (psychological continuity, the biological / animalist criterion, and Parfit's "what matters");
  - the reader gives their own verdict on each case, then sees what each criterion says: yes, no, or open;
  - at the end it counts agreement with the reader's firm verdicts, leaving out "open" answers;
  - nothing is graded.
- **Uncertainty preserved:**
  - every criterion verdict restates the chapter's table, and the component adds no verdicts of its own;
  - the chapter now states plainly that cells the SEP entry does not state directly are the chapter's inference, and that the component does not mark them cell by cell;
  - a manuscript sentence that said otherwise was corrected in this cycle;
  - the "what this demo is and is not" panel says each cell is the answer on one reading.
- **Saved state:** `philopilot.v2.s4.media.identity-cases`, with a 重新开始 button. Verified to survive a reload, and verified not to change the reading position.
- **Input and accessibility:**
  - native buttons with `aria-pressed` in labelled groups;
  - checked with mouse, keyboard (visible 2px focus ring) and emulated touch;
  - no overflow at 375px;
  - no animation.
