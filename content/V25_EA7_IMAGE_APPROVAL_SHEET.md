# PhiloPilot — 自由主义、功利主义与实用主义 (EA7) image approval sheet

> Prepared 2026-11-10 for the Product Owner (V25). **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded, and there are no placeholders.** The course is fully integrated without images. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold). After approval, each file would be downloaded once, inspected, recorded in `prototype/navigation-v2/public/media/ea7/SOURCES.md` and credited in its caption. **V25 does not authorize any download**; a download needs a separate go-ahead.

Rights are given **as the source states them**. This cycle did not read the rights field on any item page, so every licence below is "not recorded". The age of a book (1859, 1878, 1903, 1907) is **not** taken as evidence of the status of an edition or its scan.

All candidates are pages of printed editions whose text layers the chapter checked in this cycle (see the chapter's 来源与权利说明). No portraits are proposed: they would add little to the arguments, and each would need its own rights check.
