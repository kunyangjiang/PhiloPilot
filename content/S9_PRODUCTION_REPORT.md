Four six-question comparisons, each labelled （本章的比较） and each answering 问题, 概念, 论证, 比较的根据, 限度, 影响还是重建:

| Comparison | Section | Where it fails (限度) |
|---|---|---|
| 荀子 and Hobbes on the origin of order | 二 | After step 4 they part: for Xunzi, order is made by the former kings and justified by their wisdom and its effects, with no role for consent; it also transforms people (化性起伪) and ranks them (分). For Hobbes, order rests on the subjects' own covenant |
| 墨子 and Rawls on who offices are open to | 三 | Mozi's reason is good government: how a ruler should employ people. Rawls's is fairness to competitors, and his fair equality of opportunity goes further, asking society to correct what birth does to the formation of ability |
| Gandhi's swaraj and positive liberty | 四 | Berlin describes positive liberty to warn against state coercion in the name of a "true self"; Gandhi's swaraj is first self-restraint, and he rejected coercive means, so the slide Berlin fears is blocked in Gandhi by his view of means and ends |
| Confucius and Ambedkar on what law can do | 五 | Both say law is not enough, but the supplement is nearly opposite: 礼 is a hierarchical order; Ambedkar demands the end of scripturally sanctioned hierarchy |

## 8. Component (`ui/media/s9/ObligationGrounds.tsx`)

- **Placement:** in 一, after the table of five grounds. The anchor is the paragraph starting "表格是本章的整理，它只列出每一种理由最常见的反驳", which itself introduces the component.
- **What it does:**
  1. The reader picks one of three people: 小林 (born here, never consented), 阿妮 (naturalised by oath), 老周 (a member of a minority the law treats unequally).
  2. For each of the five grounds the reader records 能证成 / 部分地 / 不能.
  3. When all five are recorded, the component shows what the chapter says bears on each ground for that person, lists the reader's own "yes" grounds, and, if the reader has tested other people, asks whether the same ground works equally for each. It closes with a course claim on generality and particularity, and on why philosophical anarchism is not a call to overthrow the state.
  - **Grading:** nothing is graded, and no ground is declared to succeed.
- **Ordering:** the component sits in 一, so every consideration restates 一 only. Consent points forward to 二 for tacit consent; 老周's case points forward to civil disobedience in 六. Locke and Hume, who appear only in 二, are not used.
- **Saved state:** `philopilot.v2.s9.media.obligation-grounds`. Judgements are keyed by person and ground, so switching person keeps the others. 重新开始 clears everything.
- **Checked in the browser:** all three people driven to the result, and the cross-person prompt; keyboard selection (Enter on a focused option) with a visible focus outline; no overflow at 375px; no console errors. State persistence uses the same `useSavedIx` hook as LiarResponses, which was reloaded and checked.
- **Input and accessibility:** labelled `Pick` groups, `aria-live` for results, no animation.
