## 4. Terminology and translation

- **Eight tracked terms** in the 导读 table: 天演 / 进化, 群, 自由 / 自繇, 公德 / 私德, 仁 / 通, 科学, 人生观, 哲学. Each row gives a common meaning, the usages the course follows (e.g. 科学 as a standard for 陈独秀, a method for 胡适 and 丁文江, bounded knowledge for 张君劢) and the parts where it appears.
- **Three kinds of new terms** (Part 二 table): an old word given a new sense (自由, 经济); Japanese-mediated terms (哲学, 进化, 公德); new compounds or transliterations (天演, 以太). Each assignment has a stated source or is marked as the common account (note 5).
- **严复's own words are kept** (天演, 物竞, 天择, 自繇, 群) when quoting him, glossed with the later standard terms. The course never silently "modernises" his vocabulary.
- **"冲击—回应"** is named only to be criticised (C&R 1). **"器物—制度—文化"** is used as a later summary, not as a fact about 1895.
- **"新文化运动"** is treated as a journal and a network, not as one doctrine; 梁漱溟 is placed as an interlocutor, not an opponent.
- **"科学与人生观论战"** is the course's name for the 1923 debate, after the title of the 1923 亚东 collection. "科玄论战" is given once as the name common in Chinese textbooks. The manuscript rejects the popular "science vs superstition" reading.
- **"哲学"** for pre-modern Chinese thought: the course uses it, and C&R 2 discusses the anachronism.
- **Translation of foreign names** follows the texts quoted (赫胥黎, 斯宾塞, 穆勒, 倭铿), with the modern form in a gloss where they differ.
- **Course cross-references** use registry course names, never codes.

## 5. Critiques & Responses decisions

**Included, with two critiques**, both about the course's framework rather than about individual thinkers:
1. **"冲击—回应" is too simple.** The critique: the mid-century model makes China passive and treats internal Qing developments (常州 今文经学, 考据, 王夫之's revival) as background. Response: the course absorbs part of it (康有为's progress from 公羊 三世, not evolution; 章太炎's 唯识 and 考据 resources; 丁文江's claim that scientific method equals the method of the Qing classicists). It keeps the crisis as its starting point for two reasons: the thinkers themselves saw it so (严复 "自秦以來未有若斯之亟"), and the new vocabulary really did come from outside. Open problem kept: the thinkers had reasons to exaggerate the novelty of the crisis; a two-line narrative (internal Qing scholarship and the encounter) would be better, and the course states that it mainly tells the second line.
2. **Reading these figures as "philosophy" is anachronistic.** The critique: most of them were essayists, reformers, translators and writers persuading readers to act; reconstructing their "arguments" judges them by a standard set up only in the period of Part 六. Response: the danger is real and the course marks it (Part 一 on 严复's practical aim; Part 三 on 谭嗣同 deriving history from concepts), but these writers did argue (严复's 相似 vs 真同, 陈独秀 on the 共和 / 三纲 incompatibility, 张君劢's five contrasts), and taking an argument seriously is a form of respect. Open problem kept: reconstruction is selective and drops rhetoric, emotion and strategy (谭嗣同's passion, 陈独秀's satire).

**Why only two.** The manuscript says the period is itself a chain of critiques and responses, which the body already presents (levels of diagnosis, choice of translations, 康 vs 谭, the value of progress, the limits of science, periodisation). The C&R section adds critiques of the course's own framework only. The structure (批评 / 回应 / 仍然存在的问题) matches CN6.

## 6. Visual-interactive proposals

Four candidates were considered. **Recommended: C and A. Optional: D. Rejected: B.** Nothing is implemented. Shared constraints as in CN5 / CN6: no network calls or new libraries, keyboard access, text plus colour (never colour alone), `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, and a static fallback that reads correctly without JavaScript. Every quotation inside a component must already appear, verified, in the manuscript.

### C. 1923 论战工作台 (1923 Debate Workshop): **recommended, priority 1**

- **Placement.** Part 五, after 「这场论战争的是什么？」 and before 想一想（五）.
- **Objective.** Let the learner locate each participant's line between "what science can settle" and "what it cannot", and see that the debate was about **where** to draw that line.
- **Visual design.** A horizontal band divided into six question cards: 物质世界的规律; 心理现象; 社会和历史; 道德规范; 人生的意义; 审美和爱. Above the band, four participant tokens: 张君劢, 丁文江, 梁启超, 陈独秀 (胡适 as an optional fifth). Each token draws a vertical boundary line on the band.
- **Interaction.** Step 1: the learner places their own boundary (a draggable divider, or arrow keys). Step 2: "看他们怎么划" reveals each participant's line, one at a time, with one verified sentence from the manuscript. Step 3: the learner may move their line again.
- **Feedback.** A one-line reason for each participant's line, taken from the manuscript's positions table. A closing note: the four lines disagree on two cards above all (道德规范, 人生的意义), which is the real disagreement.
- **Safeguards.** Ungraded. No "winner" label. Each participant gets one quotation of similar length. 丁文江's sentence is labelled 转引自胡适《丁文江的传记》. 陈独秀's position (人生观 is caused by social and economic conditions) is shown as a third kind of answer, not as a point on the same line, by a separate marker below the band.
- **Desktop / mobile.** Desktop: the band is horizontal, with tokens above. Mobile: the band becomes a vertical list of six cards with a "可以由科学解决 / 不能" toggle on each, and the participant lines become badges on the cards.
- **Minimal React / SVG approach.** One component, static data `{id, name, cutIndex, quote, cite, note}`. The band is an SVG of six `<rect>`s with `<line>` markers; the divider is a native `<input type="range">` for accessibility, styled over the band. State: `learnerCut`, `revealed: Set<id>`. About 140 lines.

### A. 翻译实验室 (Translation Lab): **recommended, priority 2**

- **Placement.** Part 二, after the three-kinds-of-new-terms table.
- **Objective.** Show that choosing a translation is choosing an interpretation: 严复 vs later standard terms, and what each choice brings in.
- **Visual design.** Five rows, one per concept: evolution, natural selection, liberty, society, philosophy. Each row has the English word, 严复's term (天演, 天择, 自繇, 群, —) and the later standard term (进化, 自然选择, 自由, 社会, 哲学). Between them is a small "connotation" panel.
- **Interaction.** The learner taps a candidate term; the panel shows (a) where the term came from (classical / Japanese-mediated / new), (b) the manuscript's verified quotation on it (e.g. 译凡例 on 自由, 王国维 on 新学语, "一名之立，旬月踟躕"), and (c) one question: "这个词让读者想到什么？" A final row lets the learner compare 严复's 体用 sentence with a plain-modern paraphrase.
- **Feedback.** Descriptive only: origin, quotation and the manuscript's comment. No right answer.
- **Safeguards.** Origins marked as "common account" where note 5 says so (e.g. 西周 and 哲学). No etymology that is not in the manuscript. 严复's terms are never called "wrong"; the panel says what each term foregrounds.
- **Desktop / mobile.** Desktop: a table with an expanding side panel. Mobile: one concept per card, swiped or stepped with buttons, with the panel beneath.
- **Minimal React / SVG approach.** Plain HTML table plus `<details>`-style panel, no SVG needed. Data `{concept, en, yan, later, origin, quote, cite, comment}`. About 100 lines.

### D. 史学史浏览器 (Historiography Explorer): **optional**

- **Placement.** Part 六, after 梁启超's periodisation.
- **Objective.** Compare how 梁启超 (1902), 胡适 (1919) and 冯友兰 (1931 / 1934) cut Chinese intellectual history, and what each cut makes visible or invisible.
- **Visual design.** A single timeline (先秦 → 清) with three stacked bands, one per historian. 梁启超's band has his eight periods (with a marker noting he says 七); 胡适's band shows only the 卷上 scope (古代), since the rest was never published; 冯友兰's band shows 子学时代 / 经学时代.
- **Interaction.** Hover or tap a segment to see its label and the manuscript's comment. A toggle "这样分会看不见什么？" highlights the manuscript's two examples (魏晋 儒学经学; 戴震).
- **Safeguards.** 胡适's and 冯友兰's bands carry a visible note: "本课没有核对原书，分期依据通常的叙述". This is why D is optional: two of its three bands rest on unverified summaries.
- **Desktop / mobile.** Desktop: horizontal bands. Mobile: vertical timeline with three columns.
- **Minimal React / SVG approach.** One SVG, three rows of `<rect>`s from static data `{historian, segments:[{from, to, label, note}]}`. About 110 lines.

## 9. Academic uncertainties

1. **严复 between Huxley and Spencer.** The two-layer reading is the course's own; Schwartz 1964 was not read (note 2). The boundary between Huxley's text, 严复's rewriting and his 复案 was not checked against the English.
2. **The 器物—制度—文化 scheme** is attributed to 梁启超's 五十年中国进化概论, which was not read (note 1).
3. **Japanese mediation** of 公德, 哲学 and 进化 is the common scholarly account; no Japanese source was read (notes 3, 5).
4. **康有为's dating.** The publication history of 新学伪经考 and 孔子改制考 and the founding of 孔教会 are the common account (note 6).
5. **仁学.** Its writing and posthumous publication are not discussed in detail; one article of 界说 has a transcription error and is not quoted.
6. **章太炎's essays.** Dates (1906–1907) and the 民报 originals not checked; the 太炎文录初编 text was used (note 7).
7. **梁漱溟** is known only through three sentences quoted in 胡适's review. Part 四 therefore presents 胡适's reading of 梁漱溟, and says so (note 8). **This is the weakest point of Part 四**; a specialist may want a short paraphrase of the book from a reliable secondary source.
8. **丁文江** is quoted only through 胡适's 1956 biography; 张东荪, 林宰平 and 张君劢's long reply were not read (note 9).
9. **胡适's 大纲, 冯友兰's 中国哲学史, and the 陈寅恪 / 金岳霖 reports** were not available as checkable full texts. Part 六 summarises them from the common account and marks each one (note 11).
10. **"中国哲学的合法性"** debate (2000s) is mentioned, not read (note 11).
11. **Secondary reliance.** The scholarly frame (Fairbank's "impact–response", Schwartz on 严复) is named but not read. The course states this rather than paraphrasing those books.
12. **Length.** About 39,700 CJK characters, slightly below CN6 (about 43,400). No cuts proposed.

## 10. CN7 / CN8 boundaries

**Backward boundary.** CN7 opens where CN6 ends (戴震 and 考据). It uses CN6 concepts (格物, 体用, 程颢's 仁, 名教) and points back to that course by name. References to CN5 now say 本系列课程《魏晋玄学与中国佛学》, not "上一门课" (§3).

**Forward boundary.** CN7 ends at the moment "中国哲学" becomes a discipline, around 1930: 胡适 1919, the 1923 debate, 冯友兰's history (1931 / 1934) and its review reports. Its closing section hands three questions to the next course: (1) can Chinese philosophers build systems, not only histories; (2) what will Marxism do to the debate about science and life-views; (3) can 心性之学 be defended in modern terms.

**What CN7 deliberately leaves to CN8:**
- 冯友兰 as a system-builder (新理学) and 金岳霖 as a philosopher (CN7 mentions both only as historian and reviewer).
- 熊十力, 唐君毅, 牟宗三 (CN7 note 10 only previews 牟宗三's 坎陷).
- Marxism after 李大钊: CN7 stops at 问题与主义 and 陈独秀's 1923 preface; 艾思奇, 实践论 and 矛盾论 belong to CN8.
- 梁漱溟's later work and the 乡村建设 movement (mentioned, not taught).

**Overlaps checked.** 李大钊 我的马克思主义观 is used in both courses. CN7 cites it for the 问题与主义 context; CN8 quotes it as the starting point of Chinese Marxist philosophy. The two passages are different and serve different arguments. The 1923 debate appears in CN8 only as a back-reference (新理学 and 金岳霖 are read against it). No blockquote is repeated between the two manuscripts.

**Bridge terms.** CN7's 导读 table tracks 科学, 人生观 and 哲学; CN8's tracks 体用 (pointing back to 严复) and 心. CN8 Part 一 reads 新理学 against the 1923 debate, and Part 三 connects the 1958 宣言's third point to it.
