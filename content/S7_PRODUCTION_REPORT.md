## 5. Comparisons

Two six-question comparisons, each labelled （本章的比较） and each with a "where the analogy fails" row:

1. **Care ethics and Confucian 仁 (五).** Both start from concrete relations rather than impartial rules.
   - Where the analogy fails: Confucian relations are hierarchical and role-bound, while care ethics criticises such hierarchy and began partly as a critique of the gendered division of care work.
   - The fuller comparison is left to D4.
2. **Mencius' 四端 and Aristotle's habituation (六).** Both describe moral development from a starting point.
   - Where the analogy fails: for Aristotle the character virtues are not natural (1103a19–26, as GR3 paraphrases it), while for Mencius the sprouts are innate. The political settings also differ.
   - The row notes that Xunzi is in some ways closer to Aristotle.

Both end with "两者没有历史联系".

## 8. Component (`ui/media/s7/FrameworkCase.tsx`)

- **Placement:** in 七, after the paragraph under the table "几个框架，一个案例". The anchor is the paragraph starting "这张表格还说明了一件事".
- **What it does:**
  1. The reader picks one case: 阿梅的周六, 移植 or 转轨.
  2. The reader weighs each morally relevant feature as 很重要 / 有些重要 / 不重要. After each choice, the component names the framework that looks at that feature first.
  3. Once every feature is weighed, the component shows, for each framework, what it looks at first, what it tends toward and what it notices. It also names the frameworks whose features the reader marked 很重要, and reminds the reader that "tends toward" is not "must conclude".
  4. The reader gives a verdict and sees which frameworks the chapter says tend toward it, followed by a course claim on why the frameworks disagree.
  - **Grading:** nothing is graded.
- **Uncertainty preserved:**
  - For 移植 and 转轨, the chapter applies only consequentialism and deontology. The component says so and does not invent verdicts for virtue or care ethics.
  - Where no framework in the chapter supports a verdict ("取器官", "不可以转轨"), it says "本章没有介绍倾向于这个判断的框架" and explains why.
  - The 阿梅 claim ends with moral residue.
- **Saved state:** `philopilot.v2.s7.media.framework-case`, with a 重新开始 button. The verdict is stored per case.
- **Checked in the browser:**
  - all three cases were driven to the end;
  - 移植 and 转轨 show the "本章没有用德性伦理和关怀伦理分析" note;
  - an emulated touch tap at 375px switched the case;
  - no overflow at 375px.
- **Input and accessibility:**

  - no animation.
