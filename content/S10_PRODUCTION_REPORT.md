Structure: 导读, six numbered sections, 结语, 注释 (18 notes) and 来源与权利说明. There are six 想一想 exercises, one per section, each with a hint. Core question: "科学的证据怎样支持一个理论？当我们用"最好的说明"来推断看不见的东西时，这种推理什么时候是可靠的？"

The chapter's main methodological reminder (导读) is that philosophical criticism of inference is not a rejection of evidence: the limits of induction, falsification and success are claims about the form of reasoning, not reasons to doubt established science.

## 5. Comparisons and cases

S10 has **no formal six-question comparison**. The curriculum asks for its non-Western panels to be "treated as cases of explanation, not as curiosities", so the 导读 presents them as **historical cases**. Each case separates three layers (what the source says, how the source course interprets it, and the chapter's reconstruction with modern concepts):

- the Mohist 景到 as an explanation, not a description (二);
- the Han 物类相动 list, which puts real causes, real but misexplained phenomena and correlations side by side (二);
- Chinese medicine's correlative explanation, briefly (二);
- al-Fārābī's vial as a Duhem case: two hypotheses fit the same observation (四).

Each is marked as a reconstruction that does not attribute modern concepts to the ancient authors (本章没有核实的地方 4). Whether S10 should also carry a six-question comparison is raised in the integration report, §5.

## 8. Component (`ui/media/s10/AnomalyCase.tsx`)

- **Placement:** in 四, after the two planets. The anchor is the paragraph starting "两个故事用的是同一个办法", which ends "下面的组件请你在两个案例中各选一个要修改的假定，然后看历史上实际发生了什么".
- **What it does:**
  1. The reader picks Uranus or Mercury.
  2. The reader picks which assumption to revise: the core theory, the observations, or "an unseen planet". The component shows what that revision would predict that could be checked independently, and restates Duhem's point (read just above).
  3. A button reveals what actually happened: Neptune found in 1846; Vulcan never reliably observed and the anomaly explained by general relativity.
  4. After both planets, a course claim asks how a rescue differs from a mere evasion and points forward to Lakatos in the next subsection.
  - **Grading:** the reader's choice is never marked right.
- **Ordering:** the component precedes the Lakatos subsection. It therefore uses the chapter's own words at the anchor (核心的理论 / 辅助的假定), not Lakatos's "hard core" and "protective belt", and does not give away his reading. A draft that also included al-Fārābī's vial was cut back during validation, because the vial comes later in 四 and the 导读 promises "两颗行星".
- **Saved state:** `philopilot.v2.s10.media.anomaly-case`. Picks and reveals are kept per planet. 重新开始 clears everything.
- **Checked in the browser:** both planets driven through every step; keyboard (Enter, then Tab Tab Space) selects with a visible focus outline; reset returns the saved JSON to `INIT`; no overflow at 375px; no console errors.
