# PhiloPilot — 德国观念论与浪漫主义 (EA5) image approval sheet

> Prepared 2026-11-10 for the Product Owner (V24). **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded, and there are no placeholders.** The course is fully integrated without images. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold). After approval, each file would be downloaded once, inspected, recorded in `prototype/navigation-v2/public/media/ea5/SOURCES.md` and credited in its caption. **V24 does not authorize any download**; a download needs a separate go-ahead.

Rights are given **as the source states them**. This cycle did not read the rights field on any item page, so every licence below is "not recorded". The age of a text (1794, 1799, 1807, 1812) is **not** taken as evidence of the status of an edition or its scan. For the Lasson editions (1907–1917), the editor's death (1932) is more than seventy years ago, but that is not a statement about the scan.

All candidates are printed editions whose text layers the chapter checked in this cycle.
