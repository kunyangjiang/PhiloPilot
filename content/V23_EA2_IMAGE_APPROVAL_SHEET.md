# PhiloPilot — 近代早期理性主义与新科学 (EA2) image approval sheet

> Prepared 2026-11-10 for the Product Owner (V23). **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded, and there are no placeholders.** The course is fully integrated without images. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold). After approval, each file would be downloaded once, inspected, recorded in `prototype/navigation-v2/public/media/ea2/SOURCES.md` and credited in its caption. **V23 does not authorize any download**; a download needs a separate go-ahead.

Rights are given **as the source states them**. This cycle did not read the rights field on any item page, so every licence below is "not recorded". The age of a text (1641, 1643, 1677, 1714) is **not** taken as evidence of the status of an edition or its scan.

All candidates are printed editions whose text layers the chapter checked in this cycle.
