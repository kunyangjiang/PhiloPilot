## 2. Central philosophical arguments (worked examples)

- **Track A thesis:** NE X.7–8 rank two *lives*, not two components of happiness, so they do not by themselves imply that moral activity has no place in the best life. The argument rests on βίος in 1177b26–31 and 1178a5–10, and on the masculine article in 1178a9, which makes "the life according to the other virtue" the one called second.
  - Weakest step (3), named: a life organised around one activity also contains others.
  - Objection (the dominant reading): 1177b26–31 also ranks *activities*.
  - Revision: the chapters rank both activities and lives, but a higher activity does not exclude a lower one from the best life.
- **Track B thesis:** 13.18 and the opening of the *Euthyphro* answer different questions. One is about where 直 lies; the other is about what piety is and whether Euthyphro knows it. So neither can directly support or refute the other.
  - Weakest step (4), named: it may overstate the difference.
  - Objection: the relatives' view in 4d–e functions like 亲亲相隐.
  - Revision: Confucius's closest counterpart is the relatives, not Socrates, yet the relatives argue mainly from fact and piety, unlike Confucius.
- **Both worked examples are philosophically substantive.** Each makes a falsifiable thesis, rests on checkable Greek or Chinese text, names its weakest step, and meets an objection from the actual scholarly debate: the dominant vs inclusive readings via SEP and GR3, and the 亲亲互隐 debate via CN1 and Deng Xiaomang 2007. Each ends with a thesis that is smaller but more defensible. Neither is presented as a model answer.

## 4. Source verification and limitations

- **New Greek, verified against Perseus this cycle:**
  - NE 1177b26–31 and 1178a5–10 (Bywater 1894; tlg0086.tlg010.perseus-grc2), including "ὁ κατὰ τὸν νοῦν βίος … δευτέρως δʼ ὁ κατὰ τὴν ἄλλην ἀρετήν".
  - *Euthyphro* 4b, 4b–c, 4d–e and 4e (Burnet; tlg0059.tlg001.perseus-grc1). The digital text's ὑὸν is kept and annotated (note 7).
- **Reused text, confirmed by script:**
  - Analects 13.18 is byte-identical to CN1, which also records the 直躬 / 德躬 variant (Li Rui 2024).
  - "δευτέρως δʼ ὁ κατὰ τὴν ἄλλην ἀρετήν" matches GR3.
- **Read this cycle:** SEP "Aristotle's Ethics" §10 (rev. 2026-08-11).
- **Second hand only:** Ackrill 1974, Kraut 1989, Hardie; Deng Xiaomang 2007 and the wider 亲亲互隐 debate (via CN1); Li Rui 2024.
- **Gaps.** 1178b5–7 is used through GR3's paraphrase only (note 1). The grammatical note on the article in 1178a9 is the chapter's own and should be reviewed by a classicist.

## 5. Comparative methodology

Track B applies D1's rules as its own method:

- Fix the question before comparing.
- Do not treat similarity as influence.
- Identify *who* on each side is the right counterpart.

The revised thesis is itself a comparative-method result: the correct comparand is the relatives, not Socrates. Track A shows that a single-tradition project needs the same discipline about voices and evidence.

## 8. Component (`ui/media/d11/ProjectPlanner.tsx`)

- **Anchor:** in 五, after the paragraph beginning "第 8 部分特别重要".
- **What the reader does:** chooses a track, writes one or two sentences for each of the eight parts, optionally shows the chapter's worked example for that track beside each part, and ticks the self-check questions. The two comparison items appear only for Track B.
- **No grading:** a counter says how many parts have text, and that this is "一个提示，不是分数". Ticking a check means "I asked myself honestly", not "the answer is yes".
- **Rationale:** the brief asks for an optional planner, and the eight-part structure is the chapter's final product.
- **What it is not:** the planner does not stand in for the project. The chapter, not the component, carries the method. The component asks only for one or two sentences per part, and its limits note says that the examples are not model answers.
- **Storage:** writing is stored only in this browser (`philopilot.v2.D11.media.project-planner`). Nothing is submitted or collected. A reset button clears it.
