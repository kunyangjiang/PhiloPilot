# PhiloPilot — 从古代晚期到经院哲学 (EA1) image approval sheet

> Prepared 2026-11-10 for the Product Owner (V23). **Nothing has been downloaded or embedded, and there are no placeholders.** The course is fully integrated without images. Each candidate needs an explicit decision (approve / reject / hold). After approval, each file would be downloaded once, inspected, recorded in `prototype/navigation-v2/public/media/ea1/SOURCES.md` and credited in its caption. **V23 does not authorize any download**; a download needs a separate go-ahead.

Rights are given **as the source states them**. This cycle did not read the rights field on any item page, so every licence below is "not recorded". The age of a text (Augustine, Boethius, Anselm, Aquinas) is **not** taken as evidence of the status of a nineteenth-century edition or its scan.

All candidates are printed editions whose text layers the chapter checked in this cycle. They show where each quoted Latin sentence comes from.
