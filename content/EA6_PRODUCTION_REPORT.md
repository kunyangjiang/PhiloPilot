## 2. Important arguments

- **Feuerbach:** "the consciousness of God is the self-consciousness of man". This is an explanation of origin, not a disproof. Still, a complete explanation that does not need the belief to be true weakens the grounds for holding it.
- **Marx against Feuerbach:** the human essence is the ensemble of social relations (Thesis 6).
- **Marx on Hegel:** Hegel's dialectic must be turned right side up. This is a transformation that credits Hegel, not a refutation. Fetishism continues the projection model.
- **Theory of history:** Cohen's three theses and the readings that resist "economic determinism".
- **Kierkegaard:** the spheres are not a ladder. Climacus's definition of truth separates "what" from "how", with objective uncertainty as a condition. The critique of the System is existential: it is not a refutation of Hegel's logic.
- **Schopenhauer:** the double knowledge of the body; will as groundless thing in itself; pessimism as a metaphysical conclusion; three ways out.
- **Nietzsche:** the value of values; ressentiment; internalisation; the ascetic ideal. Genealogy is "revealing", not refuting. Perspectivism denies view-from-nowhere knowledge, not better and worse perspectives.

## 10. V24 final corrections

- **Schopenhauer.** "Der Wille als Ding an sich … schlechthin grundlos" is in §23, p. 128: the "§. 23" marker (OCR "F. 23.") and the §23 opening are legible before it, and the §24 marker after it. The pendulum sentence is in §57, p. 352, between the legible §56 and §58 markers, after the §57 opening words. Both notes and both citations now give section and page.
- **1859 Preface.** Found in *Zur Kritik der politischen Ökonomie*, ed. Kautsky, Stuttgart: Dietz 1903 (author's preface pp. IX–XIII). The paraphrase was checked point by point: relations of production corresponding to a stage of the productive forces; economic structure as the real foundation with a legal and political superstructure; "es ist nicht das Bewußtsein der Menschen, das ihr Sein … bestimmt" (now quoted, one sentence, p. XI); forces in contradiction with relations, which become fetters, then an epoch of social revolution; no social formation perishes before all the productive forces it has room for are developed. The note on Cohen now says "development thesis" and "primacy thesis" are Cohen's terms, not the Preface's.
- **1844 Manuscripts.** Not verified (see §9.1). The four-aspects table stays labelled as SEP's summary.
- **Manifesto.** The chapter names the *Manifesto* once (as what nineteenth-century readers knew Marx for) and paraphrases none of its arguments, so there is nothing to verify; the appendix now says this instead of "no usable text layer".
- **Practice and components.** No question and no component changed; no question depends on the corrected locations. The `critique-sorter` anchor in §六 is untouched.
