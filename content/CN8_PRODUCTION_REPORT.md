**Texts used:** 李大钊 我的马克思主义观 (1919, simplified text); 胡适 熊十力《要在根本處注意》一文的編者附言 (独立评论, 1933-05-21); 毛泽东 实践论 (1937-07) and 矛盾论 (1937-08), **选集 text** (its notes cite the 1990 列宁全集, and the 矛盾论 题注 states the author revised it for the first edition of the 选集); 中央党校革命大批判写作组 "合二而一论"是复辟资本主义的反动哲学 (红旗, 1971-03-29); 天津市革命大批判写作小组 哲学的解放万岁！ (人民日报, 1970-12-17). Consulted, not quoted: 钟慎 把批判唯心论的斗争进行到底 (人民日报, 1971-10-15).

**Why so few quotations.** Two independent reasons, both stated in the 导读 and the sources section:
1. **Copyright.** Most of the philosophers died after 1970 (冯友兰 1990, 金岳霖 1984, 贺麟 1992, 唐君毅 1978, 牟宗三 1995, 徐复观 1982, 李泽厚 2021, 冯契 1995; several contemporary scholars are living). Their works are protected in mainland China (50 years after death). The course does not quote them.
2. **Availability.** For 熊十力 (d. 1968) and 艾思奇 (d. 1966), whose works are public domain in China, no checkable full text was found, so they are paraphrased too.

**What replaced quotation.** Each paraphrase names its basis in a note: SEP *Modern Confucianism* (Dec 2023); IEP *Feng Youlan* (Xiaofei Tu); IEP *Modern Chinese Philosophy* (Yih-Hsien Yu); zh-wikipedia entries for dates and bibliographies only; or "学界的通常概述", where no single source was checked. Every unread work is listed in the sources section.

**Problems caught during drafting and fixed:**
- Claims that could not be checked were removed or softened: 杨献珍's exact 1964 position (now "the course has only seen the 1970s attacks"); a closing sentence implying the Chinese strand of the series ends here (there may be a later course; it now says only that this course ends).
- **IEP discrepancies.** IEP *Modern Chinese Philosophy* gives 张东荪's death as 1962 (other sources: 1973) and dates 梁漱溟's book to 1922 (the 1921 date is used in CN7). The course uses only IEP statements consistent with other sources or not otherwise checkable, and the sources section says so.
- **zh-wiki inconsistency.** The 艾思奇 and 大众哲学 entries give 1935 and 1936 for 大众哲学 (probably serial vs book); note 10 states both.
- **Cross-course references** fixed while writing this report: six places said "上一门课《宋明理学至清代》", which is two courses back. They now say "本系列课程《宋明理学至清代：理、心与实学》". Every remaining "上一门课" in CN8 refers to 中国哲学与现代.

**Treatment of political documents.** The 1970–1971 newspaper articles are quoted as **historical political documents**, as the 导读 text-type table says, not as philosophical commentary. They are quoted in one or two sentences each, to show that the argument had been replaced by a verdict.

## 4. Terminology and translation

- **Eight tracked terms** (导读 table): 理, 体用, 道, 心, 智的直觉, 实践, 矛盾, 儒学 / 儒教.
- **"新儒家"**: Part 三 sets out three usages (narrowest: 熊十力 and his students 唐君毅, 牟宗三, 徐复观; wider: adding 梁漱溟, 马一浮, 张君劢; widest: adding 冯友兰, 贺麟, 钱穆, 方东美) and adopts the narrow one, concentrating on 唐 and 牟 because they built the most systematic arguments (note 7). The part title uses 当代新儒家.
- **"马克思主义中国化"**: introduced as a later summary of the 矛盾论 move, not as the 1937 wording.
- **"马克思主义哲学在中国" / "中国的马克思主义哲学"** are the course's terms for the field. "毛泽东思想" is not used as an analytic term anywhere in the manuscript.
- **"论证 / 应用 / 政治"** are fixed terms throughout Parts 四–五 and C&R 2.
- **"反向格义"**: given as a label used by some scholars, linked back to 格义 in 《魏晋玄学与中国佛学》.
- **Western terms** are given once with the English (intellectual intuition, apologetics, new realism) and then used in Chinese.
- **Western scholars' Chinese names** follow common usage (安靖如, 贝淡宁, 白彤东, 陈汉生, 方克涛, 刘纪璐, 万百安), with the English name at first mention.
- **Course cross-references** use registry names only.

## 5. Critiques & Responses decisions

**Included, in a different form from CN6 and CN7.** CN8's critiques are not about the course's framework. They are **three debates still in progress**, each tied to one strand of the course. Because none is settled, the course gives **no "response"**. Each debate has four parts: 问题; the critics' strongest argument; the defenders' strongest argument; 本课的处理和局限.

1. **Moral metaphysics: philosophy or apologetics?** (strand: 新儒家). Critics: 智的直觉 cannot be checked independently and depends on Kant's framework. Defenders: all metaphysics starts from uncheckable premises (Kant's "fact of reason"); the dialogue with Kant advances the question; Angle shows the concepts work outside the system. Limit: the course has not read 牟宗三, and his arguments are known for their subtlety.
2. **Chinese Marxist philosophy: philosophy or ideology?** (strand: Marxism). Critics: conclusions were fixed in advance; the 1971 article; the canonical texts are revised. Defenders: 1937 texts belong to an intra-party debate, not a state ideology; original work inside the framework (抽象继承法, 积淀, 智慧说); 1978 turned the thesis against politics. Limit: the three layers intertwine, and the 1937 lecture texts were not compared.
3. **Reconstructing Chinese thought with Western concepts: discovery or transformation?** (strand: method across the course). Critics: 理 read as 共相, 良知 as 智的直觉, and practices such as 工夫 and 礼 dropped as "not philosophy". Defenders: every understanding uses the reader's concepts; 格义 led to original Chinese Buddhism; the systems themselves show originality. Limit: the course's own "gains and losses" judgement also depends on a framework.

**Why this form.** For contemporary and living positions, giving the course's own "response" would mean taking sides on open political and philosophical questions. The form keeps both sides at equal strength and makes the course's own limits visible.

## 6. Visual-interactive proposals

Five candidates were considered. **Recommended: D and C. Rejected: A, B and E.** Nothing is implemented. Shared constraints as in CN7 §6 (no network or new libraries, keyboard access, text plus colour, reduced motion, static fallback). Any quotation in a component must already be in the manuscript and verified.

### D. 实践与矛盾浏览器 (Practice & Contradiction Explorer): **recommended, priority 1**

- **Placement.** Part 四, after 「论证、应用与政治」 and before 想一想（四）.
- **Objective.** Teach the course's key method for this part: separating a claim's **argument**, its **application** and its **political function**, using the two texts the course can actually quote.
- **Visual design.** Three stacked horizontal layers labelled 论证, 应用, 政治. Six to eight statement cards taken from the manuscript, e.g. the truth-criterion sentence, the pear, the cycle; "普遍寓于特殊"; the use against copying Soviet experience; the 1971 "一分为二" verdict; the 1978 article's thesis.
- **Interaction.** The learner places each card in a layer (tap a card, then tap a layer; or drag on desktop). "看本课的分法" then shows the course's placement and a one-line reason. A second view, "一个论断的三层", picks one claim (practice as the criterion of truth) and shows it in all three layers across 1937, 1964–71 and 1978.
- **Feedback.** Descriptive, ungraded. Cards that the manuscript says belong to more than one layer are shown as straddling two layers, with the reason.
- **Safeguards.** This is the most sensitive component in the two courses. (1) Only verified quotations already in the manuscript; Mao quotations stay at one to two sentences, as in the text. (2) A fixed note under the board: "这是本课的分析框架；在实际文本中三个层次常常交织在一起" (the C&R 2 limit). (3) No evaluative labels (no "correct", "erroneous", "reactionary"); the 1971 card is labelled as a political document with its source and date. (4) No images of political leaders inside the component.
- **Desktop / mobile.** Desktop: three wide layers with cards in a tray beneath. Mobile: one card at a time with three buttons (论证 / 应用 / 政治), then a summary list.
- **Minimal React / SVG approach.** HTML only (`<ul>` layers, `<button>` cards); an optional thin SVG timeline for the second view (three ticks: 1937, 1971, 1978). Data `{id, text, cite, date, layers: ('arg'|'app'|'pol')[], reason}`. About 150 lines.

### C. 新儒家比较板 (New Confucianism Comparison): **recommended, priority 2**

- **Placement.** Part 三, replacing or sitting next to the 「分歧的地图」 table.
- **Objective.** Show that 当代新儒家 is not one doctrine: compare 熊十力, 唐君毅, 牟宗三 and 徐复观 on the same questions.
- **Visual design.** A 4 × 5 grid. Columns: the four thinkers. Rows: 本体是什么; 用什么西方资源; 科学和民主怎样来; 是否需要形而上学; 主要批评. Each cell holds a short paraphrase from the manuscript and a source tag (SEP / IEP / 通常概述).
- **Interaction.** Select a row to compare across thinkers, or a column to read one thinker down. A toggle "只看有出处的" dims cells whose basis is "学界的通常概述", so the learner sees how much rests on what.
- **Feedback.** None graded. The source tag is the feedback: it teaches the evidential status of each claim.
- **Safeguards.** Paraphrase only, no quotations (copyright and unread texts). Every cell carries its basis. Critics' views (SEP: Fung 2021, Shi & Lin 2015) are attributed to SEP's report of them, and the note says the papers themselves were not read.
- **Desktop / mobile.** Desktop: the full grid. Mobile: choose a row, then the four cells stack vertically.
- **Minimal React / SVG approach.** A table with `aria-selected` rows and columns; data `{thinker, row, text, basis: 'SEP'|'IEP'|'common'}`. About 90 lines.

### Rejected

- **A. 体系构建器 (System Builder)**: let learners assemble 冯友兰's, 金岳霖's and 熊十力's systems from concept blocks. Rejected because all three systems are known to the course only through paraphrase; an interactive model would give an unearned look of precision to the least verified part of the course.
- **B. 逻辑工作坊 (Logic Workshop)**: exercises in 金岳霖's logic. Rejected: 金岳霖's 逻辑 was not read, and a logic exercise would teach general logic, not his philosophy. It belongs, if anywhere, in a logic course.
- **E. 当代研究浏览器 (Contemporary Research Explorer)**: a map of living scholars and positions. Rejected: almost every entry would be a paraphrase of unread work by living people. That puts both accuracy and fairness at risk, and the map would date quickly. Part 六's debates table already gives the structure in a form that is easy to correct.

## 9. Academic uncertainties

1. **Secondary reliance (the main risk).** Parts 一, 二, 三 and 六 rest on SEP, IEP, zh-wikipedia and "学界的通常概述". A domain specialist should read Parts 一–三 against 冯友兰, 金岳霖 and 牟宗三's own works before release. **This is a stronger recommendation than for any previous course.**
2. **新理学's wording** ("不著实际", the four 境界) was not checked in 新理学 (note 2).
3. **金岳霖** has no SEP or IEP entry; 论道 and 知识论 are known through paraphrase only (note 6).
4. **贺麟** is the thinnest section; no encyclopaedia covers him (note 4).
5. **熊十力**: the 文言本 (1932) vs 语体本 (1944) differences are not discussed; the 内学院 rebuttal is unverified (note 5).
6. **The 1958 宣言** was not read; its three points are the common summary (note 8).
7. **牟宗三's 三系说 and 唐君毅's 九境** are common summaries (note 8).
8. **实践论 / 矛盾论**: only the 选集 text was checked. The 1937 lecture texts and Nick Knight's work on them were not read (note 11). Their relation to Soviet textbooks and to 艾思奇 is stated as the course's judgement.
9. **杨献珍's 1964 position** is known only from 1970s attacks (note 12).
10. **The 1978 article** was not read in full (note 13).
11. **李泽厚, 冯契** and all contemporary scholars are summarised from secondary accounts (notes 14–16).
12. **IEP errors.** 张东荪's death year (1962 vs 1973) and the date of 梁漱溟's book (1922 vs 1921) differ from other sources; only consistent statements were used.
13. **Political sensitivity.** Parts 四–五 discuss the Cultural Revolution period and 1978. The text describes events from public sources and quotes political documents as documents. A reviewer should confirm that the tone is acceptable for the intended audience.
14. **Mao's copyright** expires in mainland China at the end of 2026 (50 years after 1976). Until then the short quotations rely on quotation for commentary and teaching. If release falls after 1 January 2027 the question disappears.

## 10. CN7 / CN8 boundaries

**Backward boundary.** CN8 begins around 1930, where CN7 ends (the first histories of Chinese philosophy). The 导读 states what is **not** repeated: 冯友兰's history and its review reports (CN7 Part 六); 梁漱溟's early book (CN7 Part 四); 李大钊's and 陈独秀's early introduction of historical materialism (CN7 Parts 四–五).

**How the two courses hand over:**

| Topic | CN7 | CN8 |
|---|---|---|
| 冯友兰 | Historian: 子学 / 经学 periods (summary) | System-builder: 新理学, 抽象继承法, 新编 |
| 金岳霖 | Reviewer: 中国哲学 vs 在中国的哲学 | Philosopher: 论道, 知识论 |

**No duplicated blockquotes** between the two manuscripts (checked by text). 李大钊 我的马克思主义观 is cited in both, but CN7 does not blockquote it.

**Forward boundary.** CN8 ends in the present ("这个历史还没有结束"). It does not claim to end the series. No CN9 material was started.

**Parallel production.** The two courses were written at the same time. Their 导读 term tables are separate but bridge: CN7 tracks 科学 and 人生观, which CN8 Part 一 reads 新理学 against; CN8 tracks 体用 and points back to 严复's use of it in CN7. Each course's references to the other use registry names. Shared cache files (李大钊, 胡适) were used by both, and each course cites what it quotes.
