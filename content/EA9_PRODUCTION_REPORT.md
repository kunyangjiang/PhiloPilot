## 2. Important arguments

- **Husserl:** intentionality as an intrinsic feature of experiences (not a relation between two things; LU V §§10–11); quality and matter; the epoché; the principle of all principles; horizons of perception; the life-world.
- **Heidegger:** the question of being; Dasein as being-in-the-world; ready-to-hand and present-at-hand; *das Man*; anxiety; being-towards-death and authenticity (and SEP's note that this is the most disputed part).
- **Sartre:** existence precedes essence; facticity and transcendence; Pierre's absence; bad faith; situated freedom and Sartre's later self-criticism.
- **Beauvoir:** situated freedom; ambiguity; oppression vs bad faith; woman as Other; becoming a woman.
- **Merleau-Ponty:** the incomplete reduction; the body schema; habit; pathology as a lens on normal experience.
- **Gadamer:** prejudice as condition; effective history; fusion of horizons; application.
- **Arendt:** the vita activa; plurality; natality; the two remedies.
