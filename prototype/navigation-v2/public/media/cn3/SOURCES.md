## Considered and not used

- **Mawangdui *Laozi* scans** on Commons (files from 《长沙马王堆汉墓简帛集成》,
  2014, tagged public domain). Not used: Phase 11B forbids them unless their
  reuse rights are independently established, and the rights of the modern
  publication's photography were not established.
- `File:TaiYiShengShui1.jpg` (Commons; Guodian 《太一生水》 slips photographed
  at the Hubei Provincial Museum by User:Sharkman, 2009). Identity is clear
  and could have been captioned correctly (it is not the *Laozi*), but the
  licence is GFDL / CC BY-SA 3.0, which is share-alike, not attribution-only.
- `File:Dschuang-Dsi-Schmetterlingstraum-Zhuangzi-Butterfly-Dream.jpg`
  (Commons; Lu Zhi 陸治, *Zhuangzi Dreaming of a Butterfly*, Ming, mid-16th
  century, ink on silk, 29.4 × 51.4 cm, `PD-old-100`). Not used: the
  Commons record names no holding institution, and its source page
  (asianart.com, exhibition "Taoism and the Arts of China", Asian Art Museum
  San Francisco, 2001) names none either, so identity could not be checked
  against an institutional record.
- Guodian slip photographs and other Commons images rejected in Phase 11A
  (see `content/CN3_PRODUCTION_REPORT.md`).
