### A1 · 商鞅方升 (approved; inspected and not used, see above)

- **File:** `商鞅方升 164056.jpg`, 2592 × 3840 px, 2,760,597 bytes.
- **Source:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E5%95%86%E9%9E%85%E6%96%B9%E5%8D%87_164056.jpg>
- **Institution:** Shanghai Museum, East Hall (上海博物馆东馆), per the file
  description "商鞅方升，上海博物馆东馆".
- **Object:** the Shang Yang square *sheng* measure. Its inscriptions are
  usually described as Shang Yang's standard of 秦孝公十八年 (344 BCE), with
  Qin Shi Huang's 26th-year (221 BCE) unification edict added on the base.
  This description is general knowledge, not checked against the museum's
  record; the museum accession number was not found.
- **Original / replica:** photographed in the museum's display. The Shanghai
  Museum is generally reported to hold the original, but the exhibited piece
  was not verified against a museum record. Caption conservatively
  ("上海博物馆东馆展出").
- **Photograph date:** 2025-10-09.
- **Author / licence:** Hpppp0527, own work, **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Attribution
  required: "Hpppp0527 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0"; a modified version
  (crop, resize) must be shared under the same licence.
- **Intended placement:** 一 · 「商鞅变法：历史记载」, after the 史记 account
  (standard measures as part of the reforms).
