## Embedded

### sokrates-gem-81-6-81.jpg

- **Object:** *Carnelian engraved gem with a portrait of Sokrates*. Roman,
  Late Republican or Early Imperial, ca. 1st century BCE–1st century CE.
  Medium on the record: "Sard-Carnelian ?". 1.4 × 1.1 cm.
- **Date and what it shows:** made some three to four centuries after
  Socrates' death (399 BCE). It follows a later, widely copied type of
  Socrates representation; it is not a lifetime portrait, and no lifetime
  portrait of Socrates is known. The caption says this.
- **Holder:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, object number
  81.6.81, Gift of John Taylor Johnston, 1881. Record:
  <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/244952>
  (Open Access API object 244952).
- **Rights:** Public Domain. The Met Open Access API returns
  `isPublicDomain: true` for object 244952 (checked 2026-11-07). The record
  web page itself returned HTTP 429 when fetched on that date, so the rights
  status was read from the museum's own API, not from the page. The Met's
  Open Access policy makes public-domain images available for unrestricted
  use (as recorded for IN1 on 2026-11-06).
- **File:** downloaded 2026-11-07 with Product Owner approval from
  `https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/gr/web-large/DP146931.jpg`
  (the museum's "web-large" derivative). 589 × 624 px JPEG, 21,473 bytes on
  disk (the server's Content-Length header reported 21,491). Not altered (no
  crop, resize or colour change).
- **Identification:** the title, date, medium and identification as
  Socrates are the museum's. The course does not add to them.
- **Used in:** `src/ui/media/gr1/SokratesGem.tsx` (section 五), via
  `SOKRATES_GEM` in `src/ui/media/assets.ts`.

## Considered and not used

- The Met, *Marble head of a philosopher* (26.269). Not used: the record does
  not identify the sitter as Socrates.
- Medieval manuscripts of Plato or the Presocratic sources held by libraries
  that license images under non-commercial terms (e.g. CC BY-NC). Not used,
  to avoid adding non-commercial terms to the course.
