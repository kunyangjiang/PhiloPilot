## Embedded

### tonsure-of-mahavira-18-104-4.jpg

- **Object:** *Tonsure of Mahavira: Folio from a Kalpasutra Manuscript*.
  India (Gujarat). Ink, opaque watercolor and gold on paper,
  11.1 × 25.7 cm (4 3/8 × 10 1/8 in.).
- **Date:** 1461 (Samvat 1519), as given on the record. The painting is about
  two thousand years later than Mahāvīra; the caption says it shows how a
  fifteenth-century Jain workshop pictured the scene, not what he looked like.
- **Holder:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, object number
  18.104.4, Rogers Fund, 1918. Record:
  <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/37840>
  (Open Access API object 37840).
- **Rights:** Public Domain. The record page shows "Public Domain", and the
  Open Access API returns `isPublicDomain: true`; the page states that Open
  Access public-domain images "are available for unrestricted commercial and
  noncommercial use without permission or fee" (checked 2026-11-06).
- **File:** downloaded 2026-11-06 with Product Owner approval from
  `https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/as/web-large/DP154781.jpg`
  (the museum's "web-large" derivative). 599 × 268 px JPEG, 66,954 bytes.
  Not altered (no crop, resize or colour change).
- **Identification:** the title, date, place and medium are the museum's.
  The course does not identify the individual figures in the painting beyond
  the museum's title.
- **Used in:** `src/ui/media/in1/MahaviraFolio.tsx` (section 四), via
  `MAHAVIRA_FOLIO` in `src/ui/media/assets.ts`.
